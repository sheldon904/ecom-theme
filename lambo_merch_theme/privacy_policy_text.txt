9. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA)
If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). This section applies solely to California residents.
Categories of Personal Information We Collect
We collect the following categories of personal information as defined by the CCPA:
•	Identifiers (e.g., name, address, email, IP address)
•	Commercial information (e.g., products purchased, order history)
•	Internet activity (e.g., browsing history, interactions with our website)
•	Geolocation data (derived from your IP address)
•	Inferences (e.g., preferences based on purchase history)
How We Use and Share Personal Information
We use and disclose this information for the business purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including processing transactions, providing customer service, improving our website, marketing (if you have consented), and complying with legal obligations.
We do not sell your personal information.
Your Rights Under CCPA
California residents have the right to:
•	Know: Request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, use, disclose, and share.
•	Delete: Request the deletion of your personal information.
•	Opt Out of Sale: We do not sell personal information; however, you have the right to direct us not to sell your personal information if this practice changes.
•	Non-Discrimination: You have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising your CCPA rights.
Exercising Your Rights
You or an authorized agent may submit a verifiable consumer request by contacting us:

•	Mailing Address: 2027 Mayport Road, Loft A, Jacksonville, FL 32233
When you submit a request, we will verify your identity to protect your information. You may need to provide your name, email, and recent order information.
